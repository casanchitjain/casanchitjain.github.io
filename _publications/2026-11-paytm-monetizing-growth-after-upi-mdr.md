When India permits a selective merchant discount rate (MDR) on UPI payments, Paytm's CFO must decide how much of the new economics to retain, share with merchants, or reinvest, weighing near-term profitability against merchant retention in a market with PhonePe, Google Pay, and Pine Labs. The case supports discussion of two-sided platform pricing, regulatory adaptation, and strategic finance.
